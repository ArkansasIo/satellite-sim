## Two theories/scenarios

### Scenario A — Lean MVP
5–7 people, approximately 9–15 months, approximately $1.8M–$3.1M total planning envelope including non-personnel costs.

### Scenario B — Full Commercial
12–20 people, nominal 17-person team, approximately 18–24 months, preferred planning envelope of $6M–$10M.

These are estimates for planning and should be replaced by actual offers, contracts, payroll assumptions, hardware quotes, and infrastructure forecasts before financial commitment.
