# Development Credits Policy

Every release should identify project contributors, source-code contributors, historical research sources, third-party libraries, datasets, community research implementations, licenses, and required notices.

Every historically significant emulator component should have a source-register entry. A source used to understand an instruction is not automatically an author of the simulator implementation.

The project must not imply that NASA, JPL, the DSN, or the Voyager mission team developed, reviewed, endorsed, or certified the simulator without explicit evidence.

CI should validate SemVer, generated build metadata, third-party notices, and source-register references.
