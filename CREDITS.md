# Development Credits

## Project

satellite-sim is an independent simulation and research software project.

Primary developer: ArkansasIo
Repository: ArkansasIo/satellite-sim

## Research and historical sources

The Voyager simulation is based on publicly available NASA, JPL, NASA/PDS, and other historical technical material. Source citations are maintained in:
- docs/voyager-reproduction-research.md
- voyager/docs/source-register.md
- docs/voyager-ground-system.md

Historical source material is not treated as project-owned software.

## Third-party software

Before incorporation into a release, every third-party library, tool, dataset, emulator implementation, or documentation-derived asset must be recorded with its version, license, copyright holder, source, usage role, and required attribution.

## Community implementations

Public emulator projects may be used as research cross-checks. Their source code is not automatically copied into this repository. Licenses and provenance must be checked before incorporation.

## NASA/JPL attribution

NASA/JPL documents and historical technical material are research sources. The project does not claim endorsement by NASA, JPL, the Deep Space Network, or the Voyager mission team.

## Contribution credits

Future releases should maintain AUTHORS and CONTRIBUTORS records and identify significant architectural contributions in release notes.

## Credit policy

Credits distinguish original development, historical research, third-party software, third-party data, external documentation, and community research.
